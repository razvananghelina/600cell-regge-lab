# Prior-art gate: anchored endpoint-counterterm equivalence

Date: 2026-08-17

Status: completed before decomposing the committed pure-momentum defects into

## 1. Exact object and complete hypotheses

Use the two already certified local canonical maps on the fixed order-24
600-cell dust carrier, after the unique physical boundary identification:

```text
Phi_p : (q,p_pre) -> (Q,p_post),   p in {even,odd}.
```

Here `q,Q` are the 30 logarithmic squared-length orbit coordinates.  Both maps
are single-valued on the accepted local branch because the complete 65 by 65
pre-Legendre Jacobian is regular.  This statement does not assume a regular
fixed-two-boundary Dirichlet problem.

The narrow equivalence hypothesis is that the maps differ only by endpoint
momentum translations generated by differentiable boundary functions:

```text
Phi_odd = V_new o Phi_even o V_old,
V_f(q,p)=(q,p+grad f(q)),
```

with signs absorbed into the definitions of the two functions.  Impose the
additional anchoring hypotheses

```text
grad f_old(q0)=0,
grad f_new(Q0)=0.                                      (A)
```

Condition (A) says that the counterterm does not redefine the already equal
pre/post momenta of the common background.  It is a substantive hypothesis,
not a consequence of the singular Dirichlet formulation.

## 2. Exact corollary

Write `Conf(Phi)` for the final-configuration component.  At fixed `q=q0`,
anchoring gives

```text
V_old(q0,p)=(q0,p)
```

for every nearby `p`, while `V_new` changes only the final momentum.  Therefore

```text
Conf(Phi_odd(q0,p)) = Conf(Phi_even(q0,p))              (B)
```

throughout their common local branch.

- **DERIVED:** one resolved violation of (B) refutes equivalence by anchored
  endpoint counterterms.
- **DERIVED:** the conclusion uses only the regular initial-value maps; the
  rank-34 Dirichlet Hessian is irrelevant.
- **STRUCTURAL:** an unanchored linear momentum shift, a general symplectic
  coordinate change, or a field redefinition mixing configurations and
  momenta is not excluded by this corollary.

## 3. Primary literature

- Dittrich and Hoehn, [*Canonical simplicial
  gravity*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1108.1974), formulate the action as a
  generator of pre/post canonical evolution.
- Dittrich and Hoehn, [*Constraint analysis for variational discrete
  systems*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1303.4294), distinguish regular canonical
  evolution maps from constrained pre/post relations.  The present test uses
  the former and does not prescribe arbitrary Dirichlet endpoints.
- Marsden, Patrick and Shkoller, [*Multisymplectic Geometry, Variational
  Integrators, and Nonlinear
  PDEs*](https://authors.library.caltech.edu/records/74nvs-vb440), derive
  canonical boundary structures from action variations.  Adding a function
  of one endpoint shifts its boundary one-form without changing the endpoint
  configuration itself.

The algebraic corollary is standard canonical mechanics.  No novelty is
claimed for it.  Application to the two committed 600-cell schedule maps is
not located in the literature; external novelty remains **OPEN**.

## 5. Framing attack and decision scope

Failure of (B) would exclude only counterterms satisfying (A).  Allowing a
nonzero background gradient changes the canonical meaning of the published
base momenta; it is not innocuous, but it remains mathematically possible and
must be labelled **OPEN**.  Moreover, even a failure of every endpoint-term
description would still not show that either bare schedule is continuum
physics.  It would strengthen the need for a preferred schedule, a genuine
improved/perfect action, or refinement convergence.

Conversely, agreement on 16 finite rays would be a **PATTERN**, not proof in a
momentum neighbourhood.
