## Recorded census

The derived five-point action is faithful and transitive.  Its stabilizer is
the exact `A4` obtained as the normalizer of an involution centralizer `V4`.
The stabilizer has:

```text
conjugacy-class sizes = 1,3,4,4
commutator subgroup   = V4
abelianization        = C3
irrep degrees         = 1,1,1,3.
```

Hence

```text
B=C(P) crossed_product A5
 ~= M5(C[A4])
 ~= M5(C)+M5(C)+M5(C)+M15(C),
dim_C B=300, centre dimension=4.
```

The three one-dimensional `A4` characters give three exact covariant
five-dimensional representations.  Their complete blind ledger is:

| stabilizer branch | `A5` restriction | crossed-product image | kernel dimension |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| trivial | `1+4` | `M5` | 275 |
| `chi` | irreducible `W5` | `M5` | 275 |
| `chibar` | irreducible `W5` | `M5` | 275 |

The group laws and covariance relations are exact in
`Q(omega)`, `omega^2+omega+1=0`.  The two nontrivial branches reconstruct the
two monomial systems found before this audit.

The minimum dimension of a faithful left `B`-module is

```text
5+5+5+15=30.
```

Thus none of the five-dimensional natural representations, nor any
ten-dimensional double made from two of them, is faithful to `B`.

The direct sum of one fundamental module from each simple block is the
canonical minimum faithful left module, but it is not a real bimodule.  Its
commutant has dimension four.  After the standard grading double its
commutant has dimension sixteen, which cannot contain the faithful
300-dimensional opposite algebra required by order zero.  Hence the
60-state doubled minimum-left carrier also fails before a Dirac operator is
chosen.
