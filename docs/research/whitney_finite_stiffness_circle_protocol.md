# Protocol: known-answer circle gate for finite trace stiffness

Date: 2026-08-11

This protocol is frozen before evaluating the finite-stiffness circle
spectrum.  It tests whether uniform sector separation is sufficient for the
correct low-energy topology.

## Complete construction

Take the unit circle divided into `N` equal oriented edges, `h=1/N`.  On each
edge retain independent local Whitney coefficients

\[
 (a_n,b_n,e_n)
\]

for its left vertex, right vertex, and oriented 1-form.  Use the exact local
Whitney metric

\[
 M_{0,h}=\frac h6
 \begin{pmatrix}2&1\\1&2\end{pmatrix},
 \qquad
 M_{1,h}=\frac1h,
\]

and the exact local weak Kähler--Dirac block generated by

\[
 d=(-1,1).
\]

At vertex `n`, the canonical trace jump is

\[
 a_n-b_{n-1}.
\]

The tested Hermitian generalized pencil is exactly

\[
 (W_h+\kappa B_h)v=zM_hv,
 \qquad \kappa>0,
\]

where `B_h=R_h^*R_h`.  There are no volume consistency fluxes, upwind terms,
or fitted coefficients.

The conforming Whitney circle has Betti numbers `(1,1)`, hence two exact
Kähler--Dirac zero modes for every `N`.

## Preregistered exact Bloch claim

In the translation-invariant momentum sector `q=0`, change the two vertex
copies to the conforming and mismatch coordinates

\[
 c=(a+b)/\sqrt2,
 \qquad m=(a-b)/\sqrt2.
\]

After mass orthonormalization and multiplication by `h`, the constant scalar
`c` is a zero block.  The `(m,e)` block is predicted to be

\[
 \begin{pmatrix}
 12\kappa&-\sqrt{12}\\
 -\sqrt{12}&0
 \end{pmatrix}.
\]

Therefore the three exact `q=0` eigenvalues should be

\[
 z_0=0,
 \qquad
 hz_\pm=6\kappa\pm\sqrt{36\kappa^2+12}.
\]

The negative branch is the would-be harmonic 1-form.  Its absolute mass is

\[
 |z_-|=
 \frac{12}{h\left(\sqrt{36\kappa^2+12}+6\kappa\right)}.
\]

These formulas are preregistered as claims to verify, not assumed results.

## Frozen asymptotic gates

Evaluate the exact formula and independent full generalized spectra for

\[
 N=8,16,32,64,128
\]

and the three non-fitted scaling laws:

1. `kappa_N=1`;
2. `kappa_N=N`, so `kappa_N h=1`;
3. `kappa_N=N^2`, so `kappa_N h=N -> infinity`.

The labels are fixed in advance:

- **DERIVED NEGATIVE** for fixed finite stiffness if `|z_-|` grows rather
  than tends to zero;
- **DERIVED NEGATIVE** for `kappa proportional to 1/h` if `|z_-|` tends to a
  nonzero constant;
- **DERIVED** singular recovery only if `kappa h -> infinity` makes
  `|z_-| -> 0`;
- record the positive stiff branch `z_+`; do not interpret its divergence as
  a physical energy without a derived time unit.

No convergence exponent will be fitted.  The exact rationalized formula will
decide all three limits.

## Independent numerical gates

For `N=8,16` and `kappa=1,2,4`:

1. build the complete `3N x 3N` matrices without Fourier reduction;
2. check Hermiticity and positive metric;
3. check that the full spectrum contains exactly one numerical zero;
4. match the two nonzero `q=0` eigenvalues to the exact formula with relative
   residual below `1e-11`;
5. independently verify that exact assembly restores two zero modes.

## Acceptance and kill boundaries

**Acceptance for fixed finite stiffness as a conforming continuum method:**
both Betti zero modes survive or converge to zero at fixed `kappa`.

**Kill boundary:** if the 1-form harmonic mode scales as `1/(kappa h)`, fixed
finite `kappa` does not recover even the known topology of `S1`.  The pure
local-weak-plus-jump pencil then requires either the singular double scaling
`kappa h -> infinity` or additional geometry-selected consistency/flux terms.

This one-dimensional counterexample would kill a general fixed-`kappa`
continuum claim.  It would not by itself prove the detailed three-dimensional
600-cell spectrum, nor would it kill other DG completions containing fluxes.
