## 1. MICHAEL A. SHERBON - Golden Ratio Geometry

### Surse:
- [Fine-Structure Constant from Golden Ratio Geometry (ResearchGate)](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/322797654_Fine-Structure_Constant_from_Golden_Ratio_Geometry)
- [HAL Science Paper](https://hal.science/hal-01174786/document)
- [SSRN Paper](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=3148761)

### Formula Sherbon:
```
alpha^(-1) = (360/phi^2) - (2/phi^3) = 137.035999168...
```

Unde:
- 360 = grade intr-un cerc
- phi = golden ratio = 1.618...
- Eroare vs CODATA: ~0.00006%

### Interpretare:
- 360/phi^2 = "golden section of 360 degrees" = 137.508...
- 2/phi^3 = corectie din diferenta g-factors electron/proton
- Bazat pe lucrarea lui Raji Heyrovska despre raza Bohr

### Observatie Critica:
Formula lui Sherbon e DIFERITA de a noastra (20*phi^4 - 2*pi*alpha).
Ambele dau rezultate bune - COINCIDENTA sau CONEXIUNE?

## 2. STERGIOS PELLIS - Poincare Dodecahedral Space

### Surse:
- [Golden Function Model (SSRN)](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/Delivery.cfm/5387893.pdf?abstractid=5387893)
- [Poincare Dodecahedral Space Solution (SSRN)](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=4316617)

### Idee Principala:
- Universul ar putea avea forma Poincare Dodecahedral Space (PDS)
- PDS e strans legat de dodecaedru si golden ratio
- Introduce "Golden Function" - o sumatie phi-scaled

### Formula Pellis:
```
alpha^(-1) = 360*phi^(-2) - 2*phi^(-3) + (3*phi)^(-5) = 137.035999164...
```

### Conexiune cu 600-cell:
- Poincare Dodecahedral Space = S^3 / Binary Icosahedral Group
- Binary Icosahedral Group = 2I = 120 elemente
- 600-cell are 120 varfuri!
- **DECI PDS SI 600-CELL SUNT STRANS LEGATE!**

## 4. GARRETT LISI - E8 Theory of Everything

### Surse:
- [Wikipedia - Exceptionally Simple Theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/An_Exceptionally_Simple_Theory_of_Everything)
- [arXiv Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/0711.0770)
- [Scientific American](https://www.scientificamerican.com/article/garrett-lisi-e8-theory/)

### Idee:
- Toate particulele si fortele din E8 (248 dimensiuni)
- E8 contine subgrupuri pentru toate cele 4 forte

### Problema:
- NU poate deriva constantele (alpha, mase)
- NU poate explica cele 3 generatii de fermioni
- Distler & Garibaldi (2009) au aratat ca teoria nu functioneaza complet

### Conexiune cu noi:
- E8 e legat de icosaedru prin McKay correspondence
- Binary icosahedral group -> E8 affine Dynkin diagram

## 7. CONEXIUNEA CU TEORIA NOASTRA

### Similaritati:
1. Toti folosesc golden ratio (phi)
2. Toti obtin erori < 0.1%
3. Interpretari geometrice similare

### Diferente:
1. Sherbon/Pellis folosesc 360 (grade), noi folosim 20 (tetraedre)
2. Sherbon/Pellis nu au "self-energy" term explicit
3. Noi avem ecuatie self-consistenta, ei au formula directa

### Intrebare Critica:
- De ce formule DIFERITE dau rezultate similare?
- E o coincidenta sau exista o conexiune mai profunda?

### Observatie Importanta:
```
360/phi^2 = 137.508...
20*phi^4 = 137.082...

Diferenta: 137.508 - 137.082 = 0.426
```
Nu sunt egale! Deci sunt abordari diferite.

## 8. POINCARE DODECAHEDRAL SPACE SI 600-CELL

### Conexiunea Matematica:
- PDS = S^3 / (Binary Icosahedral Group)
- Binary Icosahedral Group = 2I = 120 elemente
- 600-cell e tesselarea naturala a lui S^3 cu simetrie icosaedrica
- Grupul de simetrie al 600-cell = 2I x 2I = 14400 elemente

### Implicatie:
Daca PDS e relevant pentru alpha (Pellis), atunci 600-cell
ar trebui sa fie si el relevant!

Teoria noastra pe 600-cell si teoria lui Pellis pe PDS
