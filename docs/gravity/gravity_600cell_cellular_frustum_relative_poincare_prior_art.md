# Prior-art gate: are the six cellular-frustum flexes a relative Lorentz frame?

Date: 2026-08-19

## Exact object and complete hypotheses

Work in flat Minkowski space `R^(3,1)` with metric

```text
eta = diag(1,1,1,-1).
```

Fix a nondegenerate spacelike lower tetrahedron with vertices `p_i`,
`i=0,...,3`.  Let `q_i` be a nondegenerate spacelike upper tetrahedron.
Hold fixed:

1. all six intrinsic squared lengths of the upper tetrahedron;
2. the four corresponding squared strut lengths
   `<q_i-p_i,q_i-p_i>_eta`;
3. the lower tetrahedron and the vertex correspondence `p_i <-> q_i`.

The previous exact rigidity calculation proved that the resulting
fixed-bottom infinitesimal flex space has dimension six at the tested
homothetic representatives.  This mission asks what those six directions
are.  Dust, the Regge action, the 600-cell gluing and every desired physical
spectrum are absent from this local question.

For a top-edge-preserving infinitesimal displacement, test the unique rigid
motion representation

```text
delta q_i = A q_i + b,
A in so(3,1), b in R^(3,1).
```

The differentiated strut equations define the linear map

```text
C_q(A,b)_i = 2 <q_i-p_i, A q_i+b>_eta,   i=0,...,3.
```

Thus the cellular flex space should be `ker C_q`, provided the rigid-motion
representation is complete.  The precise question is not whether both
spaces happen to have dimension six.  It is:

> Is the projection `ker C_q -> so(3,1)`, `(A,b) -> A`, a canonical
> isomorphism at the repository's static and expanding representatives?

The translation block of `C_q` is the `4 x 4` matrix whose `i`-th row is

```text
2 (q_i-p_i)^T eta.
```

If this block is invertible, every infinitesimal Lorentz generator `A`
determines one and only one compensating translation `b(A)`.  In that case
`ker C_q` is the graph of a linear map over `so(3,1)`.  If it is singular,
the six-dimensional count alone does not identify the flexes with a Lorentz
frame.

## Disclosed structural warning

For the homothetic family

```text
q_i = lambda p_i + tau e_t,
```

the four strut vectors are

```text
q_i-p_i = (lambda-1) p_i + tau e_t.
```

At `lambda=1` they are all parallel.  Therefore the translation block has
rank at most one, and its determinant vanishes identically.  This already
makes a uniform Lorentz-graph interpretation doubtful.  It does not by
itself determine the rank or image of the full map `C_q`; those are frozen
for the exact test below.

For `lambda != 1`, affine independence of the four `p_i` suggests that the
four strut vectors span Minkowski space and that the translation block is
invertible.  This is a disclosed prediction, not yet an evaluated rank.

## What the primary literature establishes

### KNOWN

- Barrett's first-order Regge calculus introduces simplex dihedral angles as
  independent variables conjugate to triangle areas.  It supports adding
  first-order geometric data, but it does not derive those data from an
  underdetermined cellular edge graph: J. W. Barrett,
  [*First order Regge calculus*](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9404124).
- Area-angle Regge calculus uses areas and angles subject to local
  constraints.  The extra variables require compatibility equations:
  B. Dittrich and S. Speziale,
  [*Area-angle variables for general relativity*](https://arxiv.org/abs/0802.0864).
- The connection phase space before gluing constraints is larger than length
  Regge phase space.  Metricity requires additional gluing conditions:
  B. Dittrich and J. P. Ryan,
  [*Phase space descriptions for simplicial 4d geometries*](https://arxiv.org/abs/0807.2806).
- Twisted geometries encode intrinsic face data together with an angle
  related to extrinsic curvature, but without shape matching their local
  polyhedra do not define a consistently glued Regge geometry:
  L. Freidel and S. Speziale,
  [*Twisted geometries: A geometric parametrisation of SU(2) phase
  space*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1001.2748).
- In Lorentzian signature, secondary simplicity constraints can enforce
  shape matching and relate the gauge-invariant twist angle to a Regge
  dihedral angle.  This is a dynamical constraint result, not a consequence
  of counting six variables: F. Anza and S. Speziale,
  [*A note on the secondary simplicity constraints in loop quantum
  gravity*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1409.0836).
- Canonical simplicial evolution is generated by a discrete action and may
  introduce data that are fixed only by later constraints.  A local
  kinematic parametrization alone is not an evolution law:
  B. Dittrich and P. A. Hoehn,
  [*Canonical simplicial gravity*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1108.1974).
