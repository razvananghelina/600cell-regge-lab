## Exact question, carrier, and hypotheses

The fixed background is the already accepted three-slab homogeneous
Lorentzian Regge--dust trajectory on the regular 600-cell, with conserved
total dust mass.  Its first and shifted centered Jacobi stencils act on the
same literal 720 edge-metric carrier.  In each of the two previously disclosed

```text
A_t=-V_t,       B_t=-M_t>0,       A_t x=lambda B_t x.
```

The preceding binary-archive calculation found a rank-15 negative generalized
projector at both centered slices.  It labelled the old and shifted projectors
common and their `Gamma=M^-1 N`, `Omega=M^-1 V` leakages zero-consistent.
However, its conservative projector errors are approximately

```text
old      0.0034,
shifted  0.0159,
```

whereas the observed projector displacement and leakage are of order
`10^-7`.  Thus that calculation cannot distinguish an exactly common bundle
from a small but resolved rotation.

This mission asks only:

1. after reconstructing all three slab Hessians directly at high precision,
   do the old and shifted rank-15 generalized projectors coincide under the
   literal edge identity;
2. does each directly reconstructed projector reduce both `Gamma` and
   `Omega` at its own slice;
3. does either projector reduce the operators at the other slice, as required
   for one fixed non-autonomous recurrence carrier?

No characteristic roots, unit-circle target, spatial Laplacian, mass,
limiting speed or particle label may be inspected in this calculation.

## Why the precision question precedes the propagator

A reduced two-stencil product on one fixed configuration space is meaningful
only if the action selects that same space at both slices, or if the action
selects a transport between two different fibers.  The current error bars are
larger than the observed displacement by roughly five orders of magnitude.
Constructing a product now would silently replace an unresolved bundle by a
chosen identity trivialization.

If identity transport is refuted, a polar/direct rotation is mathematically
canonical relative to the two projectors but is only **STRUCTURAL** as a
physical transport: it is not generated by the Regge action.  It must not be
introduced inside this mission or chosen after reading a desired spectrum.
