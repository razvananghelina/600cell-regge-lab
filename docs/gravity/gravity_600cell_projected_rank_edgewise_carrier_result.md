## 1. Exact no-go for the projected-red regulator

The explicit unit-quaternion multiplication table closes with maximum source
residual approximately `5.60e-11`.  For one actual 600-cell tetrahedron, the
rotational stabilizer induces exactly the 12 even permutations `A4`; adding
quaternion conjugation gives all 24 elements of `S4`.

The three possible opposite-midpoint diagonals of the central octahedron each
have orbit size three under `A4`, and the number of fixed diagonals is zero.
Because the 600 tetrahedra form one symmetry orbit, an equivariant global
assignment would have to choose a stabilizer-fixed diagonal at one base cell.
None exists.

Therefore:

> **DERIVED NEGATIVE:** no assignment of one projected-red diagonal per
> parent tetrahedron is `H4`-equivariant.

This does not invalidate the previous homogeneous acceleration numbers.  The
four disclosed tie rules remain regulator tests, and their small scalar spread
is a valid robustness observation for that homogeneous coefficient.  It does
invalidate treating any one of those meshes as a uniquely selected carrier
for anisotropic eigenvectors.

## 6. Post-result literature reconciliation

The refined search shows that the two mathematical ingredients are more
standard than the initial narrow search suggested.

For a regular polytope, radial projection of its barycentric subdivision onto
the sphere realizes the corresponding Coxeter complex.  Thus projected
`sd K_600` is the standard spherical `H4` chamber complex, not a new
triangulation principle:

- P. Orlik, *Milnor fiber complexes for Shephard groups*, DOI
  `10.1016/0001-8708(90)90076-Y`.

The `r`-th edgewise subdivision of a barycentric subdivision is also studied
as a standard combinatorial construction, sometimes called an
`r`-colored barycentric subdivision:

- C. A. Athanasiadis, *Edgewise subdivisions, local h-polynomials and
  excedances in the wreath product*, arXiv:`1310.0521`, DOI
  `10.1137/130939948`;
- H. Edelsbrunner and D. R. Grayson, *Edgewise Subdivision of a Simplex*, DOI
  `10.1007/s004540010063`.

Tsuda and Fujiwara explicitly note that subdivisions used for geodesic
4-domes can be arbitrary, project subdivided 600-cell vertices to the
circumsphere, and then replace the cumbersome direct Regge system by an
averaged pseudo-regular model:

- R. Tsuda and T. Fujiwara, arXiv:`2011.04120`, DOI
  `10.1093/ptep/ptab079`, Section 6.

Accordingly, no novelty is claimed for barycentric, edgewise or radial
projection separately.  The search did not locate the present exact
rank-selected projected carrier used with a complete direct irregular
Regge--dust action, but search absence is not a novelty proof.  External
novelty of that later physical use remains **OPEN**.
