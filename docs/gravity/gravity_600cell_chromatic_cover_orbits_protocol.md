## 2. Exhaustive colour-cover census

Reconstruct the 600-cell counts `(120,720,1200,600)` and the full order-14400
vertex action generated by left-right quaternion multiplication and optional
quaternion conjugation.

Starting from the standard binary-tetrahedral `2T` set:

1. take its full `H4` orbit and require 25 distinct independent 24-sets;
2. solve the maximum-independent-set problem exactly with CP-SAT and require
   `OPTIMAL`, not merely feasible;
3. add exclusion constraints for all 25 orbit sets and require `INFEASIBLE`
   at cardinality 24;
4. enumerate all exact covers of 120 vertices by five of the certified sets;
5. compare the resulting covers with the left/right coset construction only
   after the exhaustive census.

No assumed list of ten covers is allowed to substitute for steps 2--4.

## 3. Independent orientation class of every symmetry

For each vertex permutation, reconstruct its real `4 x 4` orthogonal matrix
from four linearly independent vertices and require that it maps all 120
coordinates to their permuted coordinates.

Classify it by the sign of its determinant, independently of the generation
flag.  Require exactly 7200 determinant-`+1` actions and 7200 determinant-`-1`
actions, with the former equal to the non-conjugating maps and the latter to
the conjugating maps.

Only determinant-`+1` actions may identify configurations on the fixed
oriented carrier.

## 4. Degree of every colouring and every order

Orient each sorted source tetrahedron by

```text

```

and require its full integral boundary to cancel.  For every exhaustive
unordered cover, canonically sort its five cells only to obtain a reproducible
base labelling.  For every one of its `5!` orders:

- push the complete source fundamental chain to each of the five facets of
  `boundary(Delta^4)`;
- require the five degree candidates to agree exactly;
- recompute the same degree as the signed preimage count on every facet;
- require exact alternation under order parity relative to that cover's base

Report the full degree multiset, the magnitude multiset over unordered covers,
and all 1200 ordered records.  Define compatibility mechanically by

```text

```

This sign is fixed before any physical comparison.

## 5. Exact orbit census

Represent an ordered colouring as an ordered tuple of five of the 25
certified cell identifiers.  Compute, without sampling:

- proper-rotation orbits and stabilizers of the ten unordered covers;
- proper-rotation orbits and stabilizers of all positive-degree ordered
  colourings;
- the corresponding negative-degree orbits;
- full-`H4` orbits of all 1200 ordered colourings as a control;
- the action of improper symmetries on cover chirality and degree sign;
- the action of five-phase time reversal, which reverses the ordered tuple.

If improper symmetries exchange two unordered proper-rotation orbits while
reversing chromatic degree, record that statement exactly; do not call an
improper map an equivalence of a fixed oriented carrier.

## 6. Mechanical outcome

- `CHROMATIC_DEGREE_DEGENERACY`: the exhaustive cover census succeeds but any
  colouring has zero degree.
- `ONE_ORIENTED_CANONICAL_CLASS`: all controls pass, every colouring has
  nonzero degree, and all positive-degree ordered colourings form one
  proper-rotation orbit.
- `CHIRAL_COVER_AMBIGUITY`: all controls pass, every colouring has nonzero
  degree, but the positive-degree ordered colourings form more than one
  proper-rotation orbit; improper symmetries must exchange the underlying
  cover orbits and reverse the degree sign.
- `OPEN_CONTROL_FAILURE`: any carrier, solver, degree, orientation or group
  control fails.

The number and sizes of the orbits are outputs, not acceptance targets.
