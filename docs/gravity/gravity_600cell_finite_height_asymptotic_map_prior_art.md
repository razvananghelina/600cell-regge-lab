## Known external framework

Hamilton's principal function as a generating function for discrete
pre/post momenta and constraint-surface evolution is standard canonical
simplicial gravity; see Dittrich and Hoehn,
[Canonical simplicial gravity](https://arxiv.org/abs/1108.1974), especially
the discrete evolution framework around equations (2.1)--(2.3) and the
pre/post-constraint summary.

Implicit local Regge evolution was applied explicitly to a preliminary
600-cell Friedmann cosmology by Barrett, Galassi, Miller, Sorkin, Tuckey and
Williams in
[A Parallelizable Implicit Evolution Scheme for Regge Calculus](https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/9411008).

Dust-filled 600-cell Regge evolutions and their finite causal stopping points
were studied by De Felice and Fabri in
[The Friedmann universe of dust by Regge Calculus](https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/0009093)
and
[Singularities of the closed RW metric in Regge Calculus](https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/0106077).

These sources establish that repeated canonical consistency, branch loss and
causal stopping are known Regge phenomena. They do not imply indefinite
continuation or select an asymptotic ratio for the present reduced action.
