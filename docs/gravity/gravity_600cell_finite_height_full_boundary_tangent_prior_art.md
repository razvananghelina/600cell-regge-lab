## Exact object, carrier and hypotheses

The object is the differential of the canonical relation generated by the
already accepted first finite-height Lorentzian Regge--dust slab.  The
carrier is the complete fixed triangulation

```text
720 old-boundary edges + 840 internal edges + 720 new-boundary edges,
2400 four-simplices, 6240 triangles.
```

For each of the two certified H4 staircase parities, use the same bare
length-Regge action, Lorentzian angle branch, boundary term, zero
cosmological constant and conserved De Felice--Fabri point-dust mass as in
the accepted homogeneous construction.  Matter positions, masses and
world-lines are fixed.  Matter perturbations are not part of this carrier.

The background is reconstructed, rather than copied as a decimal target,
from

```text
v = 3/2,
M = mu(v),
E(q) = 4*pi*(mu(q)-mu(v)) + q*(p(q)-p(v)) = 0,
q in (9,10),
h = (p(q)-p(v))/(2*pi*mu(q)),
lambda = 1+h*q,
rho = h^2.
```

The squared edge lengths are

```text
old boundary       1,
oriented diagonal  lambda-rho,
same-vertex pole  -rho,
new boundary       lambda^2.
```

Write logarithmic signed-squared-edge coordinates as

```text
(o,x,n) in R^720 x R^840 x R^720
```

and the complete action Hessian as `K=d^2 S/d(o,x,n)^2`.  With the canonical
sign convention

```text
p_pre  = -partial_o S,
p_post =  partial_n S,
```

the internal linearized equations and the pre-momentum equation give

```text
J [[delta x], [delta n]]
  = R [[delta o], [delta p_pre]],

J = [[ K_xx,  K_xn],
     [-K_ox, -K_on]],

R = [[-K_xo, 0],
     [ K_oo, I]].
```

If and only if `J` is regular, the resulting differential is a local map

```text
T : (delta o, delta p_pre) -> (delta n, delta p_post),

delta p_post = K_no delta o + K_nx delta x + K_nn delta n,
```

between two 1,440-dimensional boundary phase tangent spaces.  The final
edge `{u+120,v+120}` is identified with the old label `{u,v}` by the literal
cellular shift.  This is a fixed combinatorial identification, not a fitted
mode matching.

## Repository non-duplication audit

The construction is not new inside the repository.  The same complete
boundary carrier, block formula and canonical controls were already
certified in
`gravity_600cell_dust_full_boundary_tangent_result.md`, but at the old
published nearly-static background with `rho=0.0102^2`.  That accepted map
is a control for signs, dimensions and symplecticity, not evidence for
regularity at the present background.

The finite-height work completed before this gate established:

- the complete first finite-height slab and its homogeneous canonical data;
- quadratic parity agreement on a 240-dimensional scale-plus-strut carrier;
- rank `239/240` for the internal equations on that restricted carrier;
- exact identification of its one-dimensional kernel as the tangent to the
  homogeneous lapse constraint, removed after imposing fixed incoming
  momentum.

None of those results determines the rank of the full `1560 x 1560` matrix
`J`.  In particular, injectivity on the restricted 240-column carrier cannot
be promoted to full Legendre regularity.

A repository search over `full boundary`, `canonical tangent`, `Jacobi
map`, `pre-Legendre`, `finite height`, `1560` and `1440` found no committed
evaluation of this full map at the present finite-height background.  The
new calculation is therefore a background transfer of an existing method,
not a newly invented operator.

## Primary prior art

- Dittrich and Hoehn, [Canonical simplicial
  gravity](https://arxiv.org/abs/1108.1974), abstract and sections 2--5,
  formulate discrete action-generated pre/post evolution using Hamilton's
  principal function.  This is the general source of the canonical relation
  used here.
- Bahr and Dittrich, [(Broken) Gauge Symmetries and Constraints in Regge
  Calculus](https://arxiv.org/abs/0905.1670), especially sections 3--6,
  explain why curvature can lift exact discrete gauge symmetries and replace
  constraints by background-dependent pseudo-constraints.  Consequently,
  full rank here need not mean that continuum gauge degrees became physical.
- Dittrich and Hoehn, [From covariant to canonical formulations of discrete
  gravity](https://arxiv.org/abs/0912.1817), sections 2--5, derive the
  canonical data and constraints directly from a discrete action.
- Dittrich, Freidel and Speziale, [Linearized dynamics from the 4-simplex
  Regge action](https://arxiv.org/abs/0707.4513), sections 2--4, relate the
  Regge Hessian and its null directions to linearized dynamics.
- Hoehn, [Canonical linearized Regge Calculus: counting lattice gravitons
  with Pachner moves](https://arxiv.org/abs/1411.5672), sections 6--11 and
  appendix B, constructs canonical linearized evolution and separates
  vertex-displacement and lattice-graviton variables around flat
  backgrounds.  Its physical mode interpretation does not transfer
  automatically to this curved dust background.
- Barrett, Galassi, Miller, Sorkin, Tuckey and Williams, [A Parallelizable
  Implicit Evolution Scheme for Regge
  Calculus](https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/9411008), implement implicit Sorkin
  evolution and include a dust-filled 600-cell test.
- De Felice and Fabri, [The Friedmann universe of dust by Regge
  Calculus](https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/0009093) and [generalized evolution
  of the 600-cell](https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/0106077), construct
  homogeneous and enlarged-variable Sorkin evolution of the dust 600-cell.
- Liu and Williams, [Regge calculus models of closed lattice
  universes](https://arxiv.org/abs/1502.03000), study homogeneous and
  mass-perturbed closed lattice universes in a different Collins--Williams
  reduction.  It is relevant context, not the present full edgewise Jacobi
  map.

Searches used the terms `600-cell Regge dust canonical evolution`,
`linearized Regge Hessian symplectic map`, `tent move pseudo constraints`,
`anisotropic 600-cell perturbations` and `Sorkin canonical momentum`.
They located the framework above but no primary source printing this exact
finite-height `1440 x 1440` map.  A search cannot prove absence; external
novelty remains **OPEN** pending a dedicated literature review.

## Framing attack and claim boundary

Symplecticity is a mandatory implementation control, not a discovery.  Full
pre-Legendre rank would establish local uniqueness for fixed incoming
canonical data on this discretization; it would not by itself count physical
degrees of freedom.  On a curved Regge background, apparent regularity can
be pseudo-constraint lifting caused by discretization.

This map connects tangent spaces at two different homogeneous geometries:
the old scale is `1` and the new scale is `lambda`.  After the fixed cellular
identification one may represent it by a square matrix, but a one-step
eigenvalue is not yet an invariant physical frequency or stability exponent.
Such an interpretation requires a transported physical norm or frame, a
gauge/curvature reduction and at least a second dynamically solved slab.

The first calculation must therefore remain target-free and stop at:

1. exact carrier and background reconstruction;
2. sectorwise pre-Legendre regularity or singularity;
3. existence and uniqueness of the forced boundary differential;
4. canonical symplecticity;
5. schedule robustness/equivalence under the frozen cellular identification.

No eigenvalue target, continuum harmonic, desired polarization count,
speed, Planck scale or particle datum is licensed by this gate.  Only the
mission-specific verifier may be run; the full suite remains excluded.
