# Prior-art and framing gate: temporal persistence of the pseudo-longitudinal defect

Date: 2026-08-19

## Exact question

On the already accepted shifted centered recurrence of the same fixed
600-cell carrier, do the two dimensionless failures of invariance of the
tangential vertex-displacement image remain resolved away from zero in both
rank-15 negative sectors?

The observables are

```text
rho_span = ||(1-P_BL) A L||_2 / ||A L||_2,
rho_comm = ||(1-P_L) B^-1 A L||_2 / ||B^-1 A L||_2.
```

Here `L` is derived from the tangential vertex-displacement image after the
action-selected conformal quotient, while `A` and `B` are the shifted
potential and kinetic shape forms.  The complete hypotheses are the frozen
600-cell geometry, the accepted shifted centered two-slab solution, the
oriented binary symmetry reduction and sectors 4 and 5, each of dimension
25 after removing the five-dimensional conformal row.

## What the literature establishes

Bahr and Dittrich, *[Broken Gauge Symmetries and Constraints in Regge
Calculus](https://arxiv.org/abs/0905.1670)*, establish that curved Regge
solutions generally do not retain exact vertex-displacement gauge symmetry;
the broken constraints become pseudo-constraints.  Dittrich and Hoehn,
*[From covariant to canonical formulations of discrete
gravity](https://arxiv.org/abs/0912.1817)*, show that exact first-class
vertex-displacement constraints occur in linearized Regge calculus on flat
backgrounds, while nonlinear corrections make them background-dependent
pseudo-constraints.  Their later *[Canonical simplicial
gravity](https://arxiv.org/abs/1108.1974)* gives the action-generated
canonical evolution framework in which this distinction belongs.

This makes a nonzero shifted defect plausible but does not calculate it for
this 600-cell recurrence.  No primary source located in the search performed
on 2026-08-19 reports these two residuals, this carrier or a two-tick census
of its two rank-15 sectors.

## Framing attack

A later recurrence is not automatically flatter, more weakly curved or more
continuum-like.  It has the same spatial resolution.  Consequently:

- this test can establish or refute **temporal persistence on two accepted
  backgrounds**;
- it cannot establish a curvature scaling law, convergence under refinement,
  recovery of continuum diffeomorphism symmetry or physical propagation;
- comparing two values cannot establish monotonicity or a limiting rate.

Calling this a curvature or refinement test would be a category error.  A
true recovery test still requires a preregistered family that changes
curvature or resolution and reconstructs the full inhomogeneous Hessian on
each member.
