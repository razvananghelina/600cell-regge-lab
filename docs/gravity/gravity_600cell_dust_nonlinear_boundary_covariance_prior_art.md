## Primary literature

- Dittrich and Hoehn, *From covariant to canonical formulations of discrete
  gravity*, <https://arxiv.org/abs/0912.1817>, show that nonlinear terms can
  break linearized discrete gauge symmetries and generate background-dependent
  pseudo-constraints.  Thus a linear covariance can fail first at quadratic
  order.
- Dittrich and Hoehn, *Canonical simplicial gravity*,
  <https://arxiv.org/abs/1108.1974>, formulate implicit action-generated
  pre/post evolution as canonical transformations under simplex moves.  This
  supplies the correct nonlinear boundary-value problem.
- Gambini and Pullin, *Consistent discretization and canonical classical and
  quantum Regge calculus*, <https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/0511096>, likewise
  obtain finite canonical transformations after solving discrete equations;
  it does not imply triangulation independence.
- Dittrich and Steinhaus, *Path integral measure and triangulation independence
  in discrete gravity*, <https://arxiv.org/abs/1110.6866>, make clear that 4D
  triangulation independence is exceptional and must be tested rather than
  presumed.

No located primary source compares the nonlinear canonical maps of these two
dust-filled 600-cell phase schedules.  External novelty is **OPEN**.

## Framing attack

A second-order difference of two maps whose values and first derivatives agree
is expected to scale quadratically even when no physical symmetry exists.
Observing order two would diagnose where covariance breaks; it would not be a
new force law.  Conversely, agreement on a finite direction list could be a
high-symmetry cancellation and cannot establish a perfect action.

The calculation must therefore:

1. freeze the complete direction/amplitude/input list before nonlinear solves;
2. solve all 65 equations rather than project onto a stationary partition or a
   fitted shape block;
3. compare complete canonical outputs under the already selected physical-edge
   map;
4. report hit fractions and distinguish `BROKEN`, `CONSISTENT` and `OPEN`;
5. retain the order-24/full-720-edge limitation explicitly.
