# Frozen audit protocol: a global five-phase 600-cell tent schedule

Date: 2026-08-12

Status at this commit: **protocol frozen after disclosed exploratory work**.
This is not a blind preregistration of the numbers below.  The purpose of the
commit is to prevent the remaining global-slab and hostile controls from being
changed after they are evaluated.

No comparison with a particle target, a coupling, a dimensional scale or the
bootstrap symbol `a1=5` is part of this protocol.

## 1. Fixed hypotheses

1. The spatial carrier is the boundary complex of the regular 600-cell on its
   120 quaternionic vertices.  Two vertices are adjacent exactly when their
   Euclidean scalar product is `phi/2`.
2. A local tent move at `v` is the already certified carrier
   `[v_old,v_new]*L_v`, where `L_v` is the icosahedral link of `v`.
3. Two moves may occupy one simultaneous phase only when their old vertices
   are nonadjacent.  The existing local verifier established that adjacent
   vertex stars overlap and nonadjacent ones do not share a tetrahedron.
4. A complete phased schedule is an ordered proper coloring of all 120
   vertices.  Replacing a vertex by its new copy preserves the abstract
   spatial boundary graph and its color.
5. For a fixed vertex order, the proposed global slab is the staircase
   triangulation: an old tetrahedron with ordered vertices
   `v1<v2<v3<v4` contributes the four 4-simplices obtained while moving
   `v1,v2,v3,v4` in that order.
6. The full spatial symmetry used for canonicity is the order-14400 `H4`
   permutation action generated by
   `q -> l q r^-1` and `q -> l conjugate(q) r^-1`.

These hypotheses construct a combinatorial and kinematic candidate.  They do
not assume that its Regge equations are stationary.

## 2. Exploratory observations already seen

The following values were inspected before this protocol was written and
must therefore not be advertised as blind discoveries:

- the standard binary tetrahedral subgroup `2T` has 24 vertices and no
  600-cell graph edge inside it;
- its five left cosets and its five right cosets give proper 5-colorings;
- the `H4` orbit of this 24-set appeared to contain 25 independent 24-sets;
- an OR-Tools CP-SAT optimization returned independence number 24, and a
  second exclusion run found no size-24 set outside those 25;
- those 25 sets appeared to have exactly ten exact covers of the 120 vertices;
- the ten unordered covers appeared to form one `H4` orbit;
- a cover stabilizer appeared to induce `A5`, not `S5`, on its five colors,
  leaving two parity classes of ordered schedules;
- one staircase slab had f-vector
  `(240,2280,6240,6600,2400)`, Euler characteristic zero, and boundary equal
  to the two 600-cell copies.

The registered verifier must reproduce these values from a fresh construction
and must fail if any differs.  Their evidential label is **DERIVED
COMPUTATIONAL** only after that verifier passes; their provenance remains
disclosed exploratory rather than blind.

## 3. Frozen hostile tests

The verifier will perform all of the following without target fitting.

### A. Graph and group reconstruction

- reconstruct `(120,720,1200,600)` from the coordinates;
- verify degree 12 and tent-conflict adjacency equal to graph adjacency;
- construct exactly 14400 distinct `H4` graph automorphisms;
- verify the standard 24-set is the closed subgroup `2T` and is independent.

### B. Exhaustive coloring census

- certify `alpha(G)=24` by integer optimization;
- generate the `H4` orbit of the standard 24-set and verify it has 25 members;
- exclude all 25 and prove that no additional independent 24-set exists;
- enumerate exact covers by five such sets and obtain the complete multiset;
- verify the number of unordered covers, their intersection design, and all
  symmetry orbit/stabilizer sizes;
- derive `chi(G)=5` from `alpha=24`, `|V|=120`, and an exhibited five-coloring.

The solver status must be `OPTIMAL` or `INFEASIBLE` as appropriate.  A timeout
or merely feasible solution is a failed check.

### C. Every ordered schedule, not one representative

For all unordered covers and all `5!` phase orders:

- verify each phase contains 24 pairwise nonadjacent vertices;
- verify every spatial tetrahedron contains four distinct colors;
- build the complete staircase slab and obtain 2400 distinct 4-simplices;
- verify every tetrahedral facet has multiplicity one or two;
- verify the multiplicity-one facets are exactly the 600 old and 600 new
  boundary tetrahedra;
- verify the slab f-vector and Euler characteristic;
- verify that the 720 old edges, 720 final edges, 720 old-new diagonals and
  120 tent poles are exactly its edges.

This test is frozen before the exhaustive 1200-schedule run.

### D. Lorentzian kinematic control

Assign squared length `a^2` to every old, final and diagonal spatial edge and
`-rho a^2`, `rho>0`, to every tent pole.  Verify directly that every one of
the 2400 4-simplex edge-Gram matrices has inertia `(-,+,+,+)` and the same
nonzero determinant as the already derived regular Lorentzian local simplex.

This proves only that the combinatorial slab admits a coherent Lorentzian
piecewise-flat metric.  The existing regular local pole equation is nonzero
on this assignment, so it is explicitly **not** a vacuum solution.

### E. Canonicity and order

- determine the `H4` orbit count of unordered covers;
- determine the induced permutation group on the five phases;
- determine the orbit count of all ordered schedules, including time reversal;
- state whether geometry selects one schedule, one equivalence class, or more
  than one inequivalent class.

No representative is called canonical merely because it was the first found.

## 4. Acceptance and kill boundaries

### Combinatorial acceptance

The schedule problem advances if all tests A--C pass.  Then five phases are
minimal and a complete global product slab exists up to the reported symmetry
ambiguity.

### Kinematic acceptance

The kinematic problem advances if test D passes.  This means only that the
slab can be Lorentzian; it supplies neither a clock scale nor an equation of
motion.

### Dynamical boundary

No claim of global dynamics is accepted unless the full internal Regge edge
equations, including all 120 tent poles and shared spatial/diagonal edges,
are solved or a canonical evolution theorem supplies them.  Local pole
stationarity at one vertex is insufficient.

The route is killed at the relevant level if:

- the coloring census is incomplete or five phases are not minimal;
- any ordered schedule fails to be a product slab with the stated boundary;
- the claimed `H4` equivalence does not hold; or
- no coherent Lorentzian metric exists on the slab.

Failure of vacuum stationarity does not refute the combinatorial schedule; it
does refute calling that metric a dynamical tick.

## 5. Interpretation discipline

- **DERIVED** will be reserved for exact combinatorics or solver-certified
  finite statements reproduced by the verifier.
- **STRUCTURAL** will label the use of a phased foliation and equivalence under
  spatial symmetry.
- **PATTERN** will label any later numerical comparison of the forced phase
  count with an independently named `5`.
- **OPEN** will label full Regge stationarity, phase-order independence of the
  action, lapse/scale selection, `c`, `G`, Planck time and Planck mass.

Only the targeted verifier will be run.  The full repository suite is outside
this protocol.
