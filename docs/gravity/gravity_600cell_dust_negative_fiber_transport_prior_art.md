# Prior-art and framing gate: transport of the negative-shape fibers

Date: 2026-08-18

## Exact question

The direct-precision result certifies the same negative stiffness rank at two
successive centered slices.  Equal rank does not imply a common subspace or a
dynamically transported subsystem.

The next question is therefore:

> Under the unique boundary-edge identification and the canonical second-slab
> tangent generated by the Regge action, does the old negative configuration
> fiber—and its natural complexified cotangent dual—map into the shifted
> negative fiber and its dual?

No fitted basis alignment is allowed.

## Primary literature checked

1. Dittrich and Höhn, [*Canonical simplicial
   gravity*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1108.1974), identify Hamilton's principal
   function as the generator of canonical discrete evolution obtained from the
   covariant action.  This supports using the already derived tangent `T_2`,
   not an independently optimized alignment, as the dynamical transport.
2. Dittrich and Höhn, [*From covariant to canonical formulations of discrete
   gravity*](https://arxiv.org/abs/0912.1817), derive canonical data and
   linearized dynamics directly from the Regge action while emphasizing that
   discretization may break continuum gauge symmetries.  It does not imply
   invariance of the present spectral fibers.
3. Davis and Kahan, [*The Rotation of Eigenvectors by a Perturbation.
   III*](https://doi.org/10.1137/0707001), bound rotations of invariant
   subspaces of Hermitian operators using their spectral separation.  This is
   the appropriate control for midpoint projectors; equality of ranks alone
   is not.

No source found studies these two rank-`15` fibers for the accepted 600-cell
dust slabs.  That search is not proof of novelty.

## Canonicity audit

The boundary mapping in the second-slab tangent is unique and already derived
from the literal gluing.  It identifies edge-type coordinates between the two
slices without a permutation search.  Directly comparing projectors after
that map is therefore **GEOMETRIC STRUCTURAL**, but not yet a statement about
dynamics.

The action supplies more: the canonical tangent

```text
T_2 = [A B; C D]
```

maps the complexified phase space at the old slice to that at the shifted
slice.  If `E` is a configuration fiber with Hermitian projector `P`, its
natural cotangent-dual fiber in the unitary symmetry coordinates has projector
`conj(P)`.  Thus the phase fiber is selected as

```text
E + E*  with projector diag(P,conj(P)).
```

This uses the canonical `q,p` pairing and the already fixed unitary irrep
bases.  It introduces no Euclidean Procrustes or polar transport.

The four leakage blocks are consequently fixed:

```text
(I-P_new)       A P_old,
(I-P_new)       B conj(P_old),
(I-conj(P_new)) C P_old,
(I-conj(P_new)) D conj(P_old).
```

Their simultaneous vanishing is sufficient for the canonical tangent to
transport the complete phase fiber.  Failure refutes this natural phase-fiber
closure; it does not prove that no more elaborate constrained lift exists.
