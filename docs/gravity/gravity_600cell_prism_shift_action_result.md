## Exact local calculation

Fix one regular edge-one tetrahedron and set

```text
(p0,p1,p2,p3)=(0,x1,x2,x3),
H=[G,a;a^T,-1],
G_ii=1, G_ij=1/2.
```

The audit constructs the exact block inverse of `H`.  For every one of the
six lateral hinges it differentiates the normalized product of the two
opposite facet conormals through second order at the origin.  It obtains

```text
cos(theta_e)=1/3,
d cos(theta_e)=0,
sum_e Hess(theta_e)=0
```

exactly in `Q(sqrt(2))`.  Individual angle Hessians are nonzero, so the last
identity is a real six-hinge cancellation rather than a constant-angle bug.

For a local edge `(i,j)`, its lateral area is

```text

```

Consequently the directly differentiated local wedge functional obeys

```text

  = acos(1/3) * K4_reduced.
```

This reproduces the local Schlaefli cancellation as a conclusion of the
symbolic calculation.
