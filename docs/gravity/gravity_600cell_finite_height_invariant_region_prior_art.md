## Primary sources checked

1. Dittrich and Höhn, *Canonical simplicial gravity*, Class. Quantum Grav. 29
   (2012) 115009, [arXiv:1108.1974](https://arxiv.org/abs/1108.1974).
   Their equations (2.4)--(2.5) use Hamilton's principal function as a
   generating function and impose pre/post momentum matching.  This is the
   general canonical framework used here.  A canonical relation generated by
   a discrete action is therefore **KNOWN**, not a novelty claim.

2. De Felice and Fabri, *The Friedmann universe of dust by Regge Calculus:
   study of its ending point*,
   [arXiv:gr-qc/0009093](https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/0009093), and
   *Singularities of the closed RW metric in Regge Calculus: a generalized
   evolution of the 600-cell*,
   [arXiv:gr-qc/0106077](https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/0106077).
   They evolve dust-filled 600-cell geometries with a Sorkin scheme and report
   a finite stopping point associated with a causality-breaking singularity.
   Thus indefinite 600-cell evolution cannot be assumed merely because a few
   finite steps exist.

3. Liu and Williams, *Regge calculus models of the closed vacuum
   Lambda-FLRW universe*, Phys. Rev. D 93, 024032 (2016),
   [arXiv:1501.07614](https://arxiv.org/abs/1501.07614),
   [DOI:10.1103/PhysRevD.93.024032](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.93.024032).
   They analyze Collins--Williams and subdivided closed FLRW models, the
   relation between Hamiltonian and evolution equations, and abrupt endpoints
   where struts become null.  Their matter content, equations and global
   variables are not the normalized dust map tested here.

4. Jercher and Steinhaus, *Cosmology in Lorentzian Regge calculus: causality
   violations, massless scalar field and discrete dynamics*,
   [arXiv:2312.11639](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.11639).
   In a spatially flat cuboidal-frustum model they derive matter-dependent
   existence inequalities, monotone expanding/contracting branches, and a
   relational Friedmann limit in a small-deficit expansion, while higher
   orders obstruct some of those properties.  This is the closest modern
   structural control found, but it has a massless scalar, different cells and
   a different canonical map.
