# Prior-art and framing gate: rational canonical-data lift

Date: 2026-08-19

This gate precedes any exact rational elimination for the proposed global
240-direction lift.

## Proposed object

On the complete variable-face flat-frustum 600-cell slab, let

```text
F f + B b = 0,
B = [E U, S],
```

where `F` is the 6000-by-3600 cell-flex block, `U` is the unsigned
720-by-120 vertex-edge incidence map with the derived factor `8 lambda`, and
`S` carries the 120 strut data.  The proposed object is the exact rational
linear lift `f = L b`, if it exists, together with a direct residual proof
`F L + B = 0`.

This is a kinematic gluing statement.  It is not a Regge equation of motion,
a canonical momentum map, a constraint algebra, or a propagator.

## What is already known externally

Vertex-based conformal variations of edge lengths on piecewise-flat two- and
three-manifolds, including their relation to the Regge scalar-curvature
functional, are established prior art: David Glickenstein,
[Discrete conformal variations and scalar curvature on piecewise flat two and
three dimensional manifolds](https://arxiv.org/abs/0906.1560).
Consequently the unsigned endpoint form is geometrically natural, but neither
its dimension nor its naturality proves compatibility with this four-
dimensional slab.

Canonical simplicial gravity already treats simplex additions/removals as
canonical transformations generated by Hamilton's principal function and
warns that apparently free data may later be fixed by constraints: Bianca
Dittrich and Philipp A. Hoehn,
[Canonical simplicial gravity](https://arxiv.org/abs/1108.1974).

For linearized four-dimensional Regge calculus on flat backgrounds, vertex
displacement symmetry and lapse/shift variables are explicit, while lattice
gravitons are a different class of variables introduced by other Pachner
moves: Philipp A. Hoehn,
[Canonical linearized Regge Calculus: counting lattice gravitons with Pachner
moves](https://arxiv.org/abs/1411.5672).  Dittrich and Hoehn also derive local
first-class constraints on arbitrary triangulated Cauchy surfaces in the flat
linearized regime and explain their deformation into pseudo-constraints
beyond it:
[From covariant to canonical formulations of discrete gravity](https://arxiv.org/abs/0912.1817).

The evolution of a 600-cell with a larger set of free variables has itself
been studied using a Sorkin evolution scheme: A. De Felice and E. Fabri,
[Singularities of the closed RW metric in Regge Calculus: a generalized
evolution of the 600-cell](https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/0106077).  Therefore
neither "600-cell evolution" nor "more free variables" is a novelty claim.

## Framing attacks fixed before calculation

1. A rational lift proves only that the proposed boundary data can be glued
   into the chosen flat-frustum carrier at first order.  It does not prove
   that all 240 directions are physical or gauge.
2. Arbitrary strut compatibility does not imply a physical clock.  Whether a
   lapse is gauge, constrained, or dynamically selected belongs to the Regge
   action/Hessian and its constraint analysis.
3. The 120 vertex-scale variables are a conformal scalar sector, not the full
   space of spatial metric perturbations and not tensor gravitons.
4. The earlier local cell-flex lift is exactly refuted.  A positive result
   must exhibit a different globally solved lift; it may not silently reuse
   the failed one.
5. Two finite-field ranks are strong diagnostics but do not prove equality
   over `Q`.  The next verifier must use exact rational arithmetic and verify
   every original equation directly.
