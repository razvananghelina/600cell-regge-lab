## Decision

`F06` does **not** collapse completely, but it survives only after a heavy
downgrade:

1. some mixing data remain usable as flavor-only organizing structure;
2. all direct physical claims about CKM/PMNS angles and CP observables remain
   conditional on an imported flavor-matrix dictionary;
3. the strong-CP claim does **not** survive as a derived physical statement.

## 1. Survives as flavor-only structure

The following ingredients survive, but only as residual flavor scaffolding and
not as derived Standard-Model observables.

### 1.1 Exact icosahedral/A5 background

The exact discrete core still contains:

- the factorization of the fiber action through `A_5`;
- the exact fiber permutation module
  \[
  1 \oplus 3 \oplus 3' \oplus 5;
  \]
- the icosahedral/golden-ratio spectral background on the 12-fiber graph.

This is enough to justify an `A_5`/golden-ratio residual-symmetry language in
the flavor sector.

### 1.2 Exact three-slot and exponent scaffold

The flavor program still has:

- three ordered family slots;
- the exact grading
  \[
  w(a,b)=5a+6b;
  \]
- the conditional `(a,b)` placement data.

So hierarchical flavor-texture language remains legitimate.

### 1.3 CKM exponent data as texture data only

The old CKM sector contains arithmetic exponent patterns such as
`(3,7,12)` and related concordance identities. These may survive only in the
following weak sense:

- as candidate exponents of hierarchical flavor textures;
- as arithmetic constraints on a possible future Yukawa ansatz;
- not as direct derivations of physical CKM matrix elements.

### 1.4 PMNS/A5 structure as residual-symmetry candidate

The old PMNS sector may still be read as:

- an `A_5`/golden-ratio residual-symmetry candidate;
- a source of TBM-like or golden-ratio-like flavor ansatze;
- not as a derived leptonic mixing matrix.

## 2. Remains conditional

The following statements may still be explored, but only if one explicitly
imports a flavor-model dictionary beyond the exact core.

### 2.1 CKM angles

Any identification of discrete exponent data with physical CKM angles requires:

1. a concrete Yukawa-texture model;
2. a unitary extraction map from texture entries to mixing angles;
3. an explicit treatment of sector-dependent normalizations and phases.

Without these, the CKM sector is only texture data.

### 2.2 PMNS angles

Any PMNS prediction requires:

1. a lepton flavor model or residual-symmetry breaking pattern;
2. a neutrino mass-matrix ansatz;
3. a dictionary from the `A_5`/golden-ratio scaffold to the physical PMNS
   matrix.

So `theta_12`, `theta_23`, `theta_13`, and any exact PMNS matrix claim remain
conditional.

### 2.3 CP phases

The old formulas
\[
\delta_{\mathrm{CKM}}=\arctan(\sqrt{5}),
\qquad
\delta_{\mathrm{PMNS}}=3\arctan(\sqrt{5})
\]
may survive only as conditional geometric-phase candidates inside an imported
flavor model. They are **not** derived observables of the exact core.

### 2.4 Jarlskog invariant

The Jarlskog invariant is even more downstream:

- it requires physical CKM angles;
- it requires a physical CKM phase;
- therefore it is conditional on all the CKM dictionary steps above.

## 3. Falls with the gauge route

The following claims do **not** survive as physical statements.

### 3.1 Strong CP as a derived QCD claim

The statement
\[
\theta_{\mathrm{QCD}}=0
\]
does not survive as part of the exact flavor program, because it is a claim
about the QCD theta angle. Without a derived gauge/QCD sector, this is not an
allowed output of flavor-only reasoning.

What does survive is much weaker:

- the arithmetic data are real;
- some candidate flavor textures may therefore be real before additional phase
  structure is introduced.

That is **not** yet a derivation of the strong-CP solution.

### 3.2 Any claim of fully derived SM mixing

The following formulations must be excluded:

- "CKM is derived from the discrete core";
- "PMNS is derived from the discrete core";
- "CP violation is derived from the discrete core";
- any statement that presents these observables as consequences of an already
  derived Standard-Model sector.

## 4. Failure criterion check

`F06` would have collapsed completely if all mixing/CP claims depended
essentially on gauge-derived input.

That does **not** happen:

- an `A_5`/golden-ratio residual-symmetry scaffold survives;
- exponent-pattern data survive as candidate texture inputs;
- the three-slot flavor scaffold survives.

But the downgrade is severe:

- no physical mixing matrix is derived;
- no CP observable is derived;
- the strong-CP claim falls.

## 5. Strongest honest statement

The strongest honest statement after `F06` is:

- the old mixing/CP sector survives only as a residual-flavor program built
  from `A_5`/golden-ratio symmetry data, three family slots, and arithmetic
  exponent patterns;
- all actual CKM/PMNS/CP observables remain conditional on an imported flavor
  matrix model;
- the strong-CP claim must be removed from the derived-physics chain.

## Decision

`F06` is closed in split form:

- `Conditional flavor statement`:
  an `A_5`/golden-ratio residual-flavor scaffold survives, together with
  exponent-pattern data that can feed a future texture model;
- `No-go warning`:
  no physical mixing matrix or CP observable is derived from the exact
  flavor-first input set alone, and the strong-CP claim does not survive as a
  derived statement.
