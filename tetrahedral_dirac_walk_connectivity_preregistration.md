# Preregistration: connectivity of the literal robust tetrahedral walk

Date: 2026-08-11

## Starting point

Commit `69447b9` established that the literal Appendix-B stages of the
published robust tetrahedral walk compose to a local unitary permutation on
the 14,400 barycentric $H_4$ chambers.

That result did not test whether the dynamics can propagate through the
whole chamber geometry.  Its only inter-chamber maps are the intrinsic
involutions $s_2$ and $s_3$.

## Complete hypothesis class

Let $T_{23}$ be the corrected literal robust translation derived in commit
`69447b9`.  Permit an arbitrary sequence of:

- $T_{23}$ and $T_{23}^{-1}$;
- arbitrary chamber-local linear operators, including position-dependent
  coins, mass coins and arbitrary mixing of all eight local components;
- arbitrary finite products and time dependence.

Do not permit an inter-chamber map other than $s_2$ or $s_3$, an added
arc, a relabelling of the published neighbour colours, or an enlarged
spatial graph.

This class is deliberately stronger than the particular Pauli coin in the
paper.  A negative result cannot be blamed on its coin angle or internal
mixing pattern.

## Exact test

1. Reconstruct the 14,400-node four-coloured $H_4$ chamber graph.
2. Compute all connected components of the support graph generated by
   colours 2 and 3.
3. Check the orbit count and orbit sizes exactly.
4. Confirm directly that the robust translation has no matrix support
   between different such orbits.
5. Prove mechanically that multiplication by any block-diagonal
   chamber-local operator preserves the same orbit-block decomposition.
6. Compare with the component count obtained when all four intrinsic colours
   are admitted; this is a geometric control, not a proposed dynamics.

## Decision boundary

- **DERIVED CONNECTIVITY NO-GO:** the $(s_2,s_3)$ support graph has more than
  one component.  Then no literal robust walk with arbitrary local coins can
  propagate across the complete 600-cell chamber carrier.
- **ROUTE ADVANCES:** the $(s_2,s_3)$ support graph is connected.

## Scope

A negative closes only the literal two-colour transplant.  It does not close
a geometry-derived three-direction schedule using the three consecutive
Coxeter bonds

\[
(s_0,s_1),\qquad(s_1,s_2),\qquad(s_2,s_3).
\]

That ordered alternative is disclosed now, before the connectivity result,
but will require a separate preregistration and verifier.  It is not part of
the present acceptance gate.
