The paper claims α comes from: 2πα² − 4a₁φ⁴α + 1 = 0

This has three coefficients:
- A = 2π (claimed: Hopf holonomy)
- B = 4a₁φ⁴ (claimed: Cayley eigenvalue product × spectral ratio)
- C = 1 (claimed: normalization)

The worry: maybe B = 4a₁φ⁴ was CHOSEN because it gives α ≈ 1/137, not because it's the unique geometric quantity.

## TASK 3: The B coefficient specifically

The claimed B = 4a₁φ⁴ = 4 × 5 × φ⁴ ≈ 137.082

But 1/α ≈ 137.036. So B ≈ 1/α to 0.03%. 

This means ANY equation of the form:
A·α² − (1/α + ε)·α + 1 = 0
gives α ≈ 1/(1/α + ε) ≈ α(1 − εα) for small ε.

So the REAL question is: how special is B = 4a₁φ⁴?

Compute: how many "natural" expressions from framework constants give a value between 136 and 138? List ALL of them.

## TASK 4: Vieta constraint

Is 1/(2π) special? It's the KK coupling on a circle of circumference 2π.
