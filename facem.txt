# EXPERIMENT: Verify c² = a₁ = 5 from 600-cell Hopf fibration spectral gaps

## Context

In the "One Integer, Three Generations" framework, Theorem 9 defines the Lorentzian operator on the 600-cell:

□ = b₁·A_fiber - A

where A is the 600-cell adjacency matrix (120×120, degree 12), A_fiber is the adjacency matrix restricted to edges within Hopf fibers, and b₁ = 6.

## What has been proven algebraically (exact, no numerics needed)

1. □ = L_full - b₁·L_fiber (the identity coefficient b₁·deg_f - deg = 6×2 - 12 = 0 vanishes exactly)
2. Since L_full = L_fiber + L_cross: **□ = L_cross - a₁·L_fiber** where a₁ = b₁ - 1 = 5
3. The spectral gap ratio λ₁(L_full)/λ₁(L_fiber) = b₁ = 6, from the algebraic identity 12 - 6φ = 6(2 - φ)
4. The wave equation □ψ = 0 has the form L_cross·ψ = a₁·L_fiber·ψ, i.e. a wave equation with c² = a₁ = 5

## What needs numerical verification

**The critical question:** Does λ₁(L_cross) / λ₁(L_fiber) = a₁ = 5 exactly?

This requires that eigenvalues add: λ₁(L_full) = λ₁(L_fiber) + λ₁(L_cross) for the same eigenvector. This holds if either:
- [L_fiber, L_cross] = 0 (they commute), OR
- The gap eigenvector of L_full is also a gap eigenvector of L_fiber

If confirmed: c² = a₁ = 5 in lattice units, meaning the speed of light is determined by the same integer a₁ that determines everything else. c = √5 = 2φ - 1.

## Task

1. **Build the 600-cell** (120 vertices on S³, adjacency at distance 1/φ, degree 12). Verify: 720 edges, 9 distinct eigenvalues all in Z[φ], multiplicities {1,4,9,16,25,36,9,16,4}.

2. **Construct a valid Hopf fibration** — 12 fibers of 10 vertices each, partitioning all 120 vertices. Each fiber is a decagonal great circle (C₁₀ cycle). Each vertex has exactly 2 fiber neighbors and 10 cross-fiber neighbors. There are 27 distinct Hopf fibrations on the 600-cell — find at least one. Method: use the binary icosahedral group 2I (unit quaternions). A fiber through vertex q consists of all vertices {q·g^k : k=0,...,9} where g is an element of 2I of order 10. Elements of order 10 in 2I have trace 2cos(π/5) = φ.

3. **Build L_fiber and L_cross** from the Hopf fibration. Verify L_full = L_fiber + L_cross exactly.

4. **Compute spectral gaps** of L_full, L_fiber, L_cross. Check:
   - λ₁(L_full) = 12 - 6φ = 2.2918... ✓ (known)
   - λ₁(L_fiber) = 2 - φ = 0.3820... (C₁₀ cycle gap)
   - λ₁(L_cross) = ? Check if = 10 - 5φ = 1.9098... = a₁·(2-φ)

5. **Check commutativity:** ||[L_fiber, L_cross]|| = ?

6. **Gap eigenvector analysis:** For each gap eigenvector v of L_full, compute ⟨v|L_fiber|v⟩ and ⟨v|L_cross|v⟩. Check if these equal (2-φ) and (10-5φ) respectively.

7. **Verify □ = L_cross - a₁·L_fiber** as matrices.

8. **Verify □ kernel dimension = 9** (Theorem 9).

9. **Repeat for all 27 Hopf fibrations** if possible, to check stability.
