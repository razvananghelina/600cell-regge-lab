- the exact endpoint ordering was known;
- every point of the preregistered grid `u=j/200` preferred round and the
  grid was strictly monotone at five converged quadrature orders;

the numerical path pattern can be promoted to a continuous statement without
silently treating a grid as a proof.

## Endpoint and decision boundary

At the smooth round endpoint, the first variation must be established before
using convexity. The allowed argument is:

1. the smooth part is the Einstein--Hilbert/de Rham `A2` first variation;
2. the transmittal and linear cone terms are precisely the internal-face and
   corner completion of that variation;
3. the exact-minus-linear cone correction is quadratic in the deficit;
4. the unit round metric is Einstein and therefore stationary after the
   frozen volume normalization.

This gives `A2_equal_volume'(1)=0`. A small floating-point residual does not.

Decision:

- **DERIVED PATH SELECTION:** the validated Taylor plus Cauchy enclosure is
  strictly negative for `A2'(u)` on cells `0,...,18`, and the validated
  second derivative is strictly positive on cell `19`; stationarity at
  `u=1` then gives `A2'(u)<0` for every `0<=u<1`.
- **REFUTED:** a certified derivative interval is strictly positive anywhere,
  or a certified value lies below the round endpoint.
- **OPEN/FAILED CERTIFICATE:** any cell contains both signs, any analyticity
  bound fails, or any spatial enclosure is nonfinite. Numerical refinement
  may diagnose the failure but may not be reported as a proof.

Even a successful certificate concerns one affine Riemannian path and one
ordinary heat coefficient. It does not select the coefficient's physical
sign, a complete cutoff action, Lorentzian time, Newton's constant, a Planck
scale or universal source coupling.
