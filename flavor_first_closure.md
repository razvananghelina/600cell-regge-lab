# Flavor-First Closure

## Verdict

The `flavor-first` program is now closed.

It does **not** fail completely, but it reaches a natural stopping point before
any matrix-texture ansatz.

## Why it stops here

After `F01`--`F06`, the surviving flavor content is:

1. an admissible post-gauge flavor input set;
2. a minimal flavor object
   \[
   \mathcal F_{\min}=(S,L,w,N);
   \]
3. an exact integer grading
   \[
   w(a,b)=5a+6b;
   \]
4. three ordered family slots;
5. only a conditional FN-like hierarchy dictionary;
6. only a conditional residual-flavor reading of mixing/CP.

What is **missing** is exactly what would be needed to justify a next step
toward observable flavor physics:

1. a derived map from `w` to physical matrix entries;
2. a derived rule for texture zeros or matrix shape;
3. a derived phase mechanism at the matrix level;
4. a derived extraction map from discrete data to CKM/PMNS observables.

Therefore any `F07` based on explicit mass/mixing textures would introduce new
choice points and would sit too close to fitting.

## Strongest honest outcome

The strongest honest conclusion of the flavor-first route is:

- the exact discrete precursor supports a nontrivial residual flavor scaffold;
- this scaffold consists of three family slots, a `Z^2` charge lattice, an
  exact grading `w=5a+6b`, and an `A_5`/golden-ratio residual-symmetry
  background;
- but no fermion mass matrix, CKM matrix, PMNS matrix, or CP observable is
  derived from these data alone.

## Program classification

The final status of the flavor-first route is:

- `partial survival`
- `hard stop before observable-level flavor model`

This is not a failure of rigor. It is the correct stopping point once the
remaining steps would require non-derived ansatze.

## Consequence for the paper

The paper should retain:

1. the exact discrete core;
2. the documented no-go theorems for the failed gauge extensions;
3. the residual flavor scaffold, explicitly labeled as such.

The paper should **not** claim:

1. derived fermion mass matrices;
2. derived CKM/PMNS observables;
3. derived CP observables from the flavor-first scaffold alone.

## Decision

`Flavor-first` is formally closed.

The project now moves to variant `A`:

- `exact discrete precursor + residual flavor scaffold + explicit no-go results
  on failed physical extensions`.
