## Exact symmetry proof

Let (D=d+d^T) be the signed incidence operator on the (4+6+4+1=15)
simplices of an abstract tetrahedron, and let (P_k) select cochain degree
(k).  The verifier constructs the signed cochain action (R(g)) of every
vertex permutation (g\in S_4) and checks exactly that

\[
R(g)D=DR(g),
\qquad
R(g)P_k=P_kR(g).
\]

Every sum and product of those generators therefore commutes with all 24
elements of (S_4).

For the regular tetrahedron, exact Whitney integration gives a control
operator with the full stabilizer of order 24.  For the barycentric flag
child, the exact strong operator

\[
D_W=d+\delta,
\qquad
\delta_k=M_k^{-1}d_k^TM_{k+1}
\]

has stabilizer of order one under the same action.  Equality
(D_W\in\mathcal A_{\rm inc}) is therefore impossible.

This argument covers arbitrary polynomial degree, degree-dependent scalar
coins and arbitrary split-step words made only from the preregistered
generators.

## Why three naive incidence steps still fail

The previous support theorem placed all 22 missing Whitney entries at Hasse
distance three.  Distance three was only a necessary condition.

The exact path census contains 24 nonincident adjacent-degree pairs:

- 12 pairs have four unsigned length-three paths;
- 12 pairs have six unsigned length-three paths.

Their oriented contributions cancel exactly in every case, so

\[
(D^3)_{xy}=0

\]

on all 24 candidates.  The initial implementation incorrectly expected four
paths for every pair; the verifier failed `8/9`, the count was corrected to
the exact `12+12` split, and the rerun passed `9/9`.  The cancellation and the
symmetry theorem were unaffected.

## Hostile framing audit

The phrase "incidence-only" is too broad unless the permitted labels are
stated.  The theorem covers only signed incidence (D) and **cochain form
degree** (P_k).

A barycentric child also carries combinatorial ancestry data: its four
vertices are barycentres of a parent vertex, edge, face and tetrahedron.  A
construction allowed to project onto those four flag types can break the
local (S_4) symmetry without inserting a phenomenological parameter.  The
present theorem does not cover that larger algebra.  Nor does it cover
Euclidean edge/volume data, Whitney mass matrices, ancillas, added arcs or a
nonorthogonal encoding.

Consequently:

- **DERIVED:** (D) and form-degree projectors cannot generate the Whitney
  operator, at any word length.
- **DERIVED:** the naive cube (D^3) cancels on every required nonincident
  position.
- **STRUCTURAL:** metric dynamics must contain information that distinguishes
  the barycentric flag; bare abstract-tetrahedron symmetry erases it.
- **OPEN:** whether ancestry-type projectors alone suffice.
- **OPEN:** a canonical local unitary factorization of the exact metric
  operator.

This narrows the next calculation sharply: add only the four derived
barycentric ancestry labels and test the algebra they generate before adding
continuous metric weights.

## Relation to existing mathematics and physics

The proof mechanism itself is standard representation theory: an algebra
generated by symmetry intertwiners remains inside the symmetry commutant.
Discrete quantum walks, Kähler--Dirac operators, Whitney forms and finite
element exterior calculus are established subjects.

What is specific here is the exact combination: the fixed barycentric child,
its rational Whitney masses, the 22 off-incidence entries, the exact `12+12`
path census and the comparison with this particular generator algebra.  No
claim of literature-level novelty is made without a dedicated search.

Physically, this is not yet a new law.  It is a clean negative constraint on
a candidate microscopic dynamics: the earlier unitary tick remains exact
combinatorial kinematics, but metric propagation needs an additional,
geometrically selected datum.
