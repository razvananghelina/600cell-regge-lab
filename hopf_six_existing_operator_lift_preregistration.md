# Preregistration: do any existing operators lift to the 936-state carrier?

Date: 2026-08-11

## Question and acceptance rule

The canonical equivariant rook operator was closed by connectedness in commit
`0c7ca39`.  Before inventing another `D`, audit every operator family already
committed in the six-fibration chain.

An operator counts as an existing Dirac candidate on the 936-state carrier
only if all of the following were defined independently of this audit:

1. a faithful representation of
   `B_R=M6(R)^2+M12(R)^2` on its carrier or an explicit canonical
   `B_R`-equivariant lift to `H_off`;
2. an explicit map into the fixed 936-state cell decomposition, not merely an
   equality or divisibility of dimensions;
3. a nonzero grading-odd component;
4. no new rectangular intertwiner, conditional expectation, basis
   identification or Schur coefficient.

Failure of any condition kills the candidate before first order or
connectedness is tested.

## Frozen exhaustive inventory

The audit is scoped to the following operator families, which exhaust the
named committed operators in the Hopf/six-label/action chain as of protocol
time.

1. The 120-state 600-cell adjacency, fibre adjacencies and wave operators
   `A`, `A_F`, `Box_F=6A_F-A`.
2. The 2640-state cochain Kähler--Dirac operator `d+d*`, its grading and
   finite spectral moments.
3. The six-label overlap operator `Phi(X)` and the 12-state doubled auxiliary
   operator `D_aux`.
4. The complete six-label Hessian `H_X` on `1^perp` and its ten-state odd
   affine double.
5. The 36-state pair-groupoid rook operator for the commutative algebra
   `C^6`, together with its 72-state standard KO6 double.
6. Left/right regular and natural covariant operators generated by the
   transformation-groupoid algebra `C(F) crossed A5`.
7. The new stabilizer class operators `u_edge`, `u_chord`, `v_ref` and all
   polynomial/spectral functions of them.

No later target or experimental script may be added to this list after the
result is seen.  A genuinely new operator will require a separate protocol.

## Frozen exact tests

For each family record:

- carrier dimension and represented algebra;
- whether the representation of `B_R` is faithful;
- kernel dimension when it is the already-derived natural quotient;
- whether an explicit committed map to `H_off` exists;
- whether the operator is generated by the left/right algebra action;
- whether its commutator with the fixed cell grading vanishes identically;
- whether its grading-odd projection is zero, nonzero, or undefined because
  no lift exists.

On `H_off`, prove directly that every operator in the algebra generated by
the left and opposite `B_R` actions preserves every central cell `(i,j)` and
therefore commutes with gamma.  This is a full algebraic statement, not a
sampled matrix test.

Independently source-audit the authoritative registered scripts and verdict
notes for an explicit coupling/lift into the 936-state carrier.  Source
absence is evidence only for this frozen file inventory; it will not be
presented as a theorem about all possible mathematics.

## Convention traps forbidden

- The pair-groupoid carrier and the minimum faithful crossed-product module
  both happen to have dimension 36.  Dimension equality does not define an
  algebra intertwiner and must not be counted as a lift.
- `D_aux` has dimension 12, equal to one simple-module dimension.  Its known
  representation is nevertheless the nonfaithful natural `M6` quotient; it
  may not be reassigned to an `M12` block.
- An element of the crossed-product algebra can have nontrivial internal
  spectrum but still be grading-even on every bimodule cell.
- A conditional expectation or Fourier transform chosen now is a new input,
  not an existing operator.

## Decision boundary

- **ADVANCE:** an inventoried family already has a faithful canonical lift and
  a nonzero odd component.  Continue immediately to its exact real-triple
  gates.
- **DERIVED REPOSITORY-STATE NEGATIVE:** zero inventoried families meet the
  acceptance rule.  Then the present theory contains no operator capable of
  repairing the connectedness failure, and the next move necessarily adds a
  new noncentral incidence tensor.

The negative is not a universal no-go against future constructions.  It is a
complete provenance boundary for what the repository currently contains.

No Hessian target, matter character, mass or coupling comparison is allowed.
Only the targeted verifier will be run; the full suite remains excluded by
user instruction.
