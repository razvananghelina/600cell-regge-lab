## Primary prior art

- Dittrich and Hoehn, [Canonical simplicial
  gravity](https://arxiv.org/abs/1108.1974), derive action-generated
  pre/post canonical evolution in simplicial gravity, including varying
  phase-space dimensions and constraints.
- Dittrich and Hoehn, [From covariant to canonical formulations of discrete
  gravity](https://arxiv.org/abs/0912.1817), show how the covariant action,
  its broken symmetries and its linearization determine the canonical
  formalism and pseudo-constraints.
- Hoehn, [Canonical linearized Regge Calculus: counting lattice gravitons
  with Pachner moves](https://arxiv.org/abs/1411.5672), identifies
  gauge-invariant lattice-graviton degrees of freedom around *flat*
  backgrounds.  The present curved dust background does not inherit that
  identification automatically.
- Dittrich, Freidel and Speziale, [Linearized dynamics from the 4-simplex
  Regge action](https://arxiv.org/abs/0707.4513), relate the Regge Hessian,
  its zero modes and linearized gravity.
- Barrett, Galassi, Miller, Sorkin, Tuckey and Williams, [A Parallelizable
  Implicit Evolution Scheme for Regge
  Calculus](https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/9411008), apply implicit Sorkin
  evolution to a dust-filled 600-cell cosmology.
- De Felice and Fabri, [The Friedmann universe of dust by Regge
  Calculus](https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/0009093) and [generalized evolution
  of the 600-cell](https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/0106077), study homogeneous
  and enlarged-variable 600-cell evolution and its causality-ending point.

The searches used the exact terms `canonical simplicial gravity`,
`linearized Regge Hessian`, `Sorkin evolution`, `600-cell dust` and
`lattice gravitons`.  They establish that the framework is not new.  They do
not establish that no source prints the present `1440 x 1440` map.  External
novelty of its numerical spectrum is **OPEN** pending dedicated review.

## Framing attack

Symplecticity is a required control, not a discovery: it follows from a
correctly differentiated regular generating function.  Conversely, an
off-unit one-step eigenvalue is not yet a physical instability.  The
background changes between slices, the next slab has not been solved, the
map may be non-normal, and no physical perturbation norm or gauge quotient
has been selected.

The order-24 decomposition is a computational theorem about this carrier,
not a fitting device.  Minimal blocks are complex.  The real symplectic form
pairs conjugate irreducible sectors, so testing each complex block against a
naive within-block transpose form would be wrong.  The preregistration must
test the correct cross-sector bilinear pairing.

No continuous `S^3` harmonic, desired degeneracy, value of `c`, Planck scale
or particle datum may enter the blind census.  A later harmonic comparison
is licensed only if the numerical map and its canonical controls survive.

Only the mission-specific verifier and its direct controls will be run; the
full repository suite will not be run.
