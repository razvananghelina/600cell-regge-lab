## The gap

The spectral action S = Tr(f(D/Lambda)) on the 600-cell simplicial complex
produces the bosonic Lagrangian (Eq. 92 in paper):

  S ~ integral sqrt(g) d^4x [ c0*Lambda^4*f4 + c1*Lambda^2*f2*R
      + c2*f0*(8/15 * G^2 + 1/3 * W^2 + 2/15 * B^2) + ... ]

where:
- c0 = 2640, c1 = 14880, c2 = 55920
- The U(1) coefficient is 2/15 = 2/(3*a1)
- The SU(2) coefficient is 1/3 = 1/N_gen
- The SU(3) coefficient is 8/15 = rank(E8)/(3*a1)
- Ratio: 8:5:2, sum = 15 = a1*N_gen

The gauge couplings in the spectral action framework satisfy:
  1/g_i^2 = c2 * f0 * k_i
where k_i are the gauge-specific fractions (2/15, 1/3, 8/15).

For SU(3): 1/g_3^2 = c2 * f0 * 8/15
For SU(2): 1/g_2^2 = c2 * f0 * 1/3
For U(1): 1/g_1^2 = c2 * f0 * 2/15

The RATIOS of couplings are determined (no f0 needed):
  g_1^2 / g_2^2 = (1/3) / (2/15) = 5/2
  g_1^2 / g_3^2 = (8/15) / (2/15) = 4

These ratios PLUS sin^2(theta_W) = g_1^2/(g_1^2 + g_2^2) should give us
constraints. Check: sin^2(theta_W) = 1/(1 + g_2^2/g_1^2) = 1/(1 + 2/5) = 5/7.
But we need 6/26 = 3/13, not 5/7!

THIS DISCREPANCY is the core issue. The spectral action with GUT normalization
gives sin^2(theta_W) = 3/8 at the GUT scale, which runs down to ~0.23 at M_Z.
Our framework gives 6/26 as an ALGEBRAIC invariant at M_Z. The spectral action
fractions 2/15, 1/3, 8/15 are derived from 600-cell geometry but they do NOT
directly produce our coupling constants.

### Task 3: Factored spectral action

The Hopf fibration decomposes S^3 = S^1 x_H S^2 (fiber x base).
The Dirac operator on a fibered manifold decomposes as:
  D = D_fiber + D_base + mixed terms

The spectral action might factorize (approximately) as:
  S(D) ~ S(D_fiber) * S(D_base)

or more precisely, the heat kernel factorizes:
  Tr(exp(-t*D^2)) ~ Tr(exp(-t*D_fiber^2)) * Tr(exp(-t*D_base^2))

4. If yes, the alpha equation might emerge from the fiber factor

### Task 4: Self-consistency approach

This system is overdetermined: 3 equations, 1 unknown.

If the system IS consistent, then f0 is fixed and all three couplings
follow from the spectral action. If it's NOT consistent, then the
spectral action fractions (2/15, 1/3, 8/15) need modification.
