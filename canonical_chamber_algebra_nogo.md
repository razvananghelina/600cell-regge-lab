# Canonical chamber convolution audit

Date: 2026-07-28

**DERIVED — Decision.** None of the proposed canonical noncommutative
algebras gives a real-even spectral triple with the already derived
\((D,\gamma,J)\) on the 120 chambers. The common obstruction occurs before
Poincare duality: the fixed geometric \(J\) does not implement the commuting
opposite convolution action, so order zero fails.

**STRUCTURAL — Scope.** This is a no-go for the candidates specified below
with their natural regular actions on the fixed carrier. It is not a theorem
excluding every imaginable noncommutative subalgebra of \(M_{120}(\mathbb C)\).

## The framing correction

**DERIVED.** The chambers are a regular \(H_3\)-set and the three typed
adjacencies are the three Coxeter generators, with pair orders \(2,3,5\).
Their sum is the fixed chamber adjacency \(D\).

**DERIVED.** The geometric real structure is central inversion, equivalently
longest-element translation. It commutes with all three adjacency
involutions. It is not the regular-representation modular conjugation
\(\delta_g\mapsto\delta_{g^{-1}}\), which would exchange left and right
convolution.

**DERIVED.** Consequently, for a self-adjoint Coxeter generator \(R_s\),
\[
J R_s^*J^{-1}=R_s.
\]
For adjacent simple reflections \(s,t\), \([R_s,R_t]\ne0\). Thus the proposed
right-algebra/left-symmetry slogan does not supply order zero with this fixed
geometric \(J\). The successful factor-swap construction on a larger tensor
arena uses a different real structure and does not transplant to this
120-dimensional carrier.

**DERIVED.** The KO6 signs in the table are properties of the fixed
\((D,\gamma,J)\): \(J^2=+1\), \(JD=DJ\), and \(J\gamma=-\gamma J\).

**DERIVED.** For the group algebra, first order also has an explicit
simple-generator double-commutator witness. One-forms are nonzero. However
\(D\) itself belongs to the represented group algebra and is a non-scalar
element commuting with \(D\), so connectedness fails.

**DERIVED.** Metric-dimension-zero orientability fails for the group-algebra
route. Products \(\pi(a)J\pi(b)J^{-1}\) remain convolution matrices and have
constant diagonal, whereas the nonconstant orientation grading is diagonal.

**DERIVED.** At \(q=1\), the Hecke algebra is exactly the failed group-algebra
case. At \(q=2\), exact integer matrices satisfy the quadratic and braid
relations and exhibit failures of both order zero and first order. This one
generic value is enough to refute a claim that the generic Hecke family
automatically works, but it is not a proof about every exceptional parameter.

**OPEN.** I do not know a parameter-uniform classification of all exceptional
Hecke specializations with this geometric \(J\). It is unnecessary for the
posed existence claim: the canonical \(q=1\) point fails, and the generic
route already has an exact nondegenerate witness at \(q=2\).

**DERIVED.** A standard parabolic generated by adjacent simple reflections
is noncommutative and fails order zero by the same witness. A singleton, or
the commuting pair, is commutative and therefore cannot provide the requested
non-abelian-capable algebra; these commutative cases also fail first order
and have zero signed pairing.

**STRUCTURAL.** The free transitive action groupoid \(H_3\ltimes H_3\) is the
pair groupoid, whose natural convolution algebra on the chamber carrier is
\(M_{120}(\mathbb C)\). Its alleged opposite algebra under geometric \(J\) is
again the full matrix algebra, so matrix units violate order zero.

**OPEN.** “A chamber groupoid algebra” without a specified arrow set,
isotropy, involution, and representation is not one candidate and cannot be
exhausted. Selecting a smaller groupoid is extra input, hence not canonical
from the chamber graph alone.

## Corrections to the two preliminary consequences

**DERIVED.** For the represented partition algebra
\(\mathbb C^{36}\), \(U(A)=U(1)^{36}\) is abelian. Its inner-fluctuation gauge
group is therefore abelian (indeed the existing verifier finds an effective
torus). A non-abelian gauge sector is excluded for that fixed algebra and
representation, not merely open.

**STRUCTURAL.** This does not exclude a non-abelian sector on the carrier
itself. Changing the algebra changes the spectral triple, and the present
audit only excludes the natural convolution/Hecke/parabolic/pair-groupoid
actions above.

**DERIVED.** The exhaustive \(2^3\) scan proves that among the particular
\(A_5\)-invariant partition algebras obtained by contracting whole typed edge
orbits, only scalars pass first order and their fluctuations vanish.

**STRUCTURAL.** It does not prove that “every gate is satisfiable only by
breaking \(A_5\) by hand” across all possible represented algebras. That
sentence overgeneralizes a no-go for one restricted family. The new
convolution candidates fail for a different reason—order zero—but an
exhaustive classification of all \(A_5\)-equivariant noncommutative
representations on this carrier remains open.

## Pairing convention

**STRUCTURAL.** Rank, determinant, antisymmetry, and Pfaffian are not reported
as zeros when order zero fails. Without commuting represented and opposite
algebras there is no \(A\)-\(A^{\mathrm{op}}\) bimodule, hence no spectral
triple \(K_0\) intersection form. The correct ledger entry is “undefined,”
not “degenerate.”

**DERIVED.** In the commutative parabolic diagnostics the pairing is defined
and is the zero antisymmetric form: rank \(0\), determinant \(0\), Pfaffian
\(0\). In general, for an antisymmetric integral form,
\(\det Q=\operatorname{Pf}(Q)^2\); therefore determinant \(4\) means
\(|\operatorname{Pf}(Q)|=2\), nondegeneracy over \(\mathbb Q\), and failure of
unimodularity over \(\mathbb Z\).

## Status

**DERIVED.** The proposed canonical group algebra is killed by order zero,
first order, orientability, and connectedness, despite nonzero one-forms.

**DERIVED.** The canonical Hecke point \(q=1\) is the same failure; \(q=2\)
also fails order zero and first order exactly.

**DERIVED.** Noncommutative standard parabolics and the pair-groupoid algebra
fail order zero.

**OPEN.** Whether some other canonically selected noncommutative algebra on
the fixed chamber carrier passes every gate is not settled by these
candidates. Therefore the defensible answer is: no proposed canonical
noncommutative candidate works, but a carrier-wide non-abelian no-go has not
been proved.
