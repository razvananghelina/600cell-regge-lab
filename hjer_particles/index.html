600-cell Particle Physics

Interactive visualization of the Standard Model emerging from 4D geometry

What you see

A 600-cell: a 4D polytope with 120 vertices and 720 edges, projected to 3D via stereographic projection and auto-rotating in 4D.

The physics

The 120 vertices split into 5 groups of 24 (called cosets). Each group forms a 24-cell - the polytope whose symmetry is D4, the gauge group of the Standard Model.

  • 8 A-type vertices (permutations of [+/-1, 0, 0, 0]) = gauge bosons
  • 16 B-type vertices (all [+/-1/2, ...]) = Higgs sector
  • 96 C-type vertices (golden ratio coords) = fermions

The edges encode forces

All 720 edges connect vertices from different cosets (proper 5-coloring). They classify into 3 gauge channels:

  • U(1): 48 edges - electromagnetic (perfect matching, confined!)
  • SU(2): 288 edges - weak force (A-C and B-C connections)
  • SU(3): 384 edges - strong force (C-C only, with plaquettes)

1 + 3 + 8 = 12 = vertex degree = dim(Standard Model gauge group)
