# Preregistration: can three incidence substeps generate the Whitney metric?

Date: 2026-08-11

## Disclosed exploratory observation

The no-go in commit `1d2db4c` shows that all 22 missing metric entries lie at
Hasse distance three.  After that result, an exploratory exact multiplication
showed that the corresponding entries of the naive signed cube \(D^3\) are
zero.  This observation is disclosed before the present protocol; the test is
an adversarial confirmation and generalization, not a blind discovery.

## Question and complete class

On the 15-simplex cochain carrier of one tetrahedron, let

\[
D=d+d^T
\]

be the signed combinatorial Hasse operator and let \(P_k\) be the four
form-degree projectors.

Consider the complete unital operator algebra generated by

\[
\{D,P_0,P_1,P_2,P_3\}.
\]

This includes arbitrary finite words, polynomials in \(D\), degree-dependent
coins and split-step sequences whose only data are oriented incidence and
form degree.  Coefficients may be arbitrary; hence failure cannot be blamed
on choosing the wrong polynomial degree or scalar.

It does not include Euclidean coordinates, lengths, areas, Whitney mass
matrices, a distinguished flag label, added carrier states or other geometric
data.

No phenomenological target will be used.

## Exact tests

1. Reconstruct the signed action of all 24 vertex permutations \(S_4\) on
   every cochain degree.
2. Check that \(D\) and all \(P_k\) commute with those signed actions.
   Consequently every word in the generated algebra is \(S_4\)-equivariant.
3. Enumerate every length-three signed Hasse path between the 24
   degree-adjacent nonincident simplex pairs.  Record unsigned path counts and
   signed sums, equivalently the corresponding entries of \(D^3\).
4. Independently integrate the exact Whitney masses on the barycentric flag
   child and form the full strong coefficient operator

   \[
   D_W=d+\delta,\qquad
   \delta_k=M_k^{-1}d_k^TM_{k+1}.
   \]

5. Compute its exact stabilizer among the same 24 signed \(S_4\) actions.

## Decision boundary

- **DERIVED NO-GO FOR INCIDENCE-ONLY SUBSTEPS:** the generator algebra is
  fully \(S_4\)-equivariant while \(D_W\) is not.  Then no polynomial degree,
  no three-step word and no form-degree-dependent incidence construction can
  equal the anisotropic Whitney operator.
- **ROUTE REMAINS OPEN:** the metric operator is \(S_4\)-equivariant, or a
  generator fails equivariance.

This does not prohibit a geometry-aware three-step construction.  A negative
result says that unequal path weights must be derived from metric data; they
cannot emerge from incidence and form degree alone.
