# S01 No-Branching Self-Reference Theorem

Scop: sa vedem daca exista o axioma mai tare decat

- `productive = non-invertible`

dar inca suficient de curata, care sa forteze Fibonacci.

## Ideea

Contraexemplele Ising si `Rep(S_3)` arata exact ce merge prost:

- in `X \otimes X` apare un simplu suplimentar distinct de `X`.

Deci axioma candidata trebuie sa excluda exact acest fenomen.

## Candidate Axiom: No-Branching Self-Reference

Fie `C` o categorie monoidala rigida, semisimpla, generata de un simplu
netrivial `X`.

Impunem:

> **NB.** Every simple summand of `X \otimes X` is isomorphic either to
> `\mathbf{1}` or to `X`.

Adica:

\[
X \otimes X \cong a\,\mathbf{1} \oplus b\,X
\]

cu `a,b \in \mathbb{Z}_{\ge 0}`.

Aceasta este forma exacta a ideii:

- self-reference does not branch into a second nontrivial simple type.

## Theorem 1

> **Theorem (No-branching implies Fibonacci, conditional form).**
> Let `C` be a fusion category over an algebraically closed field of
> characteristic 0. Assume:
> 1. `C` is generated by one nontrivial simple object `X`;
> 2. `X` is not invertible;
> 3. `\mathbf{1}` occurs in `X \otimes X`;
> 4. every simple summand of `X \otimes X` is isomorphic either to
>    `\mathbf{1}` or to `X`.
>
> Then
> \[
> X \otimes X \cong \mathbf{1} \oplus X.
> \]

## Proof

By assumption,

\[
X \otimes X \cong a\,\mathbf{1} \oplus b\,X
\]

for some integers `a,b >= 0`.

Because `\mathbf{1}` occurs in `X \otimes X`, rigidity gives

\[
\operatorname{Hom}(\mathbf{1}, X \otimes X)
\cong
\operatorname{Hom}(X^*, X),
\]

so `X^* \cong X`; in particular, `X` is self-dual.

Now, because `X` is simple and the category is rigid,

\[
\dim \operatorname{Hom}(\mathbf{1}, X \otimes X^*)
=
\dim \operatorname{Hom}(X, X)
= 1.
\]

Since `X^* \cong X`, the multiplicity of `\mathbf{1}` in `X \otimes X` is
exactly one. Therefore

\[
X \otimes X \cong \mathbf{1} \oplus b\,X.
\]

Now the fusion subcategory generated by `X` has exactly two simple isoclasses,
namely `\mathbf{1}` and `X`, because the tensor square introduces no others and
all higher tensor powers remain in their span. Hence we have a rank-2 fusion
category.

By Ostrik's classification of rank-2 fusion categories, the only non-pointed
possibility is Fibonacci. Since `X` is not invertible, the pointed case is
excluded. Therefore

\[
b = 1,
\]

hence

\[
X \otimes X \cong \mathbf{1} \oplus X.
\]

## What This Achieves

Acum avem un theorem conditional curat:

- daca putem justifica `no-branching self-reference`,
- atunci Fibonacci urmeaza riguros.

Deci o mare parte din `S01` este redusa la o singura intrebare exacta:

- de ce ar trebui ca `X \otimes X` sa nu introduca un simplu nou diferit de `X`?

## What This Does Not Achieve

Nu am derivat inca axioma `NB`.

Prin urmare, acest document nu inchide `S01`.

El doar reduce `S01` la un miez mult mai precis.
